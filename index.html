Get the app

Three steps on any phone.

Android phones Your phone

Get it on Google Play

  1. Tap Get it on Google Play, or search “SJAI Job Hub” in the Play Store.
  2. Tap Install, then Open.
  3. Builders: tap Create your business account. Crew and customers: open the link your builder sent you.

Available in the UK and Ireland. Pro is paid through Google Play on Android, or on the website for everyone else.

iPhone and iPad Your phone

Add it from Safari — no App Store needed

You are inside Instagram, Facebook or TikTok. Tap the ··· menu and choose Open in Safari (or Open in browser) first.

  1. In Safari, open Job Hub sign-up and create your business account. Crew and customers: open your builder’s link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add. Job Hub now has its own icon and opens full screen.

Add to Home Screen only works from Safari. Allow notifications when Job Hub asks, so you see new messages.

Site teams and customers

  2. Open it. You only see the job or jobs you are on.
  3. Optional: install the app or add it to your Home Screen, so it is one tap away.

Customers only ever see their own job, and only the documents the builder chooses to share.

Questions

Is it available now?

Yes. Android phones can install it from Google Play (UK and Ireland). iPhone and iPad users add it to their Home Screen from Safari. Any builder can create an account and start free.

Is there an App Store version for iPhone?

Not yet. On iPhone you open Job Hub in Safari and tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It gets its own icon and opens full screen, like any other app.

Does my crew or my customer need an account?

No. They open the link you send them. They can install the app or add it to their Home Screen if they want it one tap away.

Who can see my jobs?

Only the people you give a link to, and only the job or jobs you assign them. Customers see their own job and no one else's. Prices, invoices and balances stay with you unless you choose to share them.

Do I need a card to start?

No. Every new account gets 14 days of Pro free, then moves to the Free plan unless you choose to upgrade on the website.

Does it check payments with my bank?

No. A customer can tell you they have paid, but you check your bank and mark the payment as received yourself.
